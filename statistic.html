Demographics &
Population

Total Population: As of the 2024 census, Tarlac has a population of 1,568,162 people. Previously, the 2020 census recorded 1,503,456 residents.
Population Density: Approximately 513.5 inhabitants per square kilometer (based on 2024 figures).
Age Distribution: Tarlac has a relatively young population with a median age of 25.4 years. The working-age group (15 to 64 years old) makes up the majority at about 63.58%, while young dependents account for 30.82%, and senior citizens make up 5.60%
