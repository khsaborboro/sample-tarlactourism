01 — Get Moving

Eight Ways To Explore

Para Motor

BAMBAN

Soar above Tarlac's fields and rivers with a professional guide at the controls — wide-open spaces and favorable weather make this a signature Tarlac experience for first-time flyers and thrill-seekers alike.

ATV Adventure

STA. JULIANA, CAPAS

Traverse muddy trails, rocky paths, and river crossings shaped by Mt. Pinatubo's 1991 lahar flows — a ride through the province's natural and volcanic history. ▶ Tap to watch

Kayaking

SAN JOSE

Tarlac Recreational Park's calm lake is perfect for a leisurely paddle or a friendly race with family — beginner-friendly, and a light workout with a view.

Mt. Pinatubo Trek

CAPAS

Trek to the famous crater lake formed by the 1991 eruption — a trail that's become one of the country's most iconic dayhikes, with river crossings along the way.

Mt. Damas Traverse

SAN CLEMENTE

Steep paths reward hikers with waterfalls and panoramic views on this challenging day circuit — a favorite among Tarlac's more experienced trekkers.

Mountain Biking

SAN JOSE

Ride scenic trails through Monasterio de Tarlac's forests and rolling hills, leading up to the iconic 30-foot Christ statue overlooking the province.

Go-Kart Racing

TARLAC REC. PARK

Take a few laps on the track at Tarlac Recreational Park — an easy, family-friendly add-on after a morning of kayaking or camping.

Zipline

TARLAC REC. PARK

Glide over the park's greenery on the zipline — a quick adrenaline hit that rounds out a day of outdoor activities at the recreational complex.

02 — Before You Go

Good to know

Book ahead

ATV and paramotor slots fill up on weekends — arrange with a certified local operator before you arrive.

Dress for the trail

Closed-toe shoes and quick-dry clothing for lahar crossings, mud, and river wading on the Pinatubo route.

Start early

Pinatubo and Damas treks begin at dawn to beat the midday heat — coordinate pickup times with your guide.

Best season

Dry months make river crossings safer and trails firmer — check conditions before a rainy-season visit.
