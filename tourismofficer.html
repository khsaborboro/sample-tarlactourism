02 — Key Responsibilities to Highlight

Tourism Officers

A Local Tourism Officer is the designated public servant responsible for championing the cultural, historical, and natural heritage of their specific municipality or city. Serving as the primary point of contact for local tourism, they orchestrate the promotion of destinations, manage visitor relations, and ensure the sustainable development of local attractions. They serve as the vital bridge between their Local Government Unit (LGU) and the Provincial Tourism Office, working collaboratively to put Tarlac on the map.

Strategic Promotion

Showcasing local festivals, eco-tourism sites, food heritage, and cultural landmarks to both domestic and foreign visitors.

Data Management

Gathering and reporting monthly visitor arrivals and accommodation statistics to help drive data-informed provincial tourism strategies.

Cultural Preservation

Acting as the local custodians of arts, culture, and historical narratives within their specific towns.

Visitor Assistance

Serving as the official point of contact for inquiries, tourism guidelines, and local travel coordination.
